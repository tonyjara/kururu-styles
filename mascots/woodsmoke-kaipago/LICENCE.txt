WHAT WAS CHANGED

The download (monkey_swing0_smol.png) is 2x art on an opaque green ground,
drawn for a game called Monkey Munch. It was halved with nearest neighbour,
which is lossless since every run in it is even, and its two greens were made
transparent. This mascot is the grey monkey's face icon, which comes in two
expressions, 15x19 each at 1x: mouth shut (x 21, y 1) and mouth open
(x 22, y 187).

  - The three rows of neck stub at the bottom are cut off, so the head is
    15x16 and the trim is exactly 16: kururu draws it at the 16px badge
    without resampling a pixel.
  - Each face is placed in the middle of a 32px cell, at the same place in
    every frame of both clips.

  row 0   idle     (8 frames, 3200ms)  mouth shut, with one blink at frame 7;
                                       the blink is the two eye pixels set to
                                       the face colour, the only pixels redrawn
  row 1   working  (4 frames, 480ms)   shut, open, shut, open: the game's munch

The colours are the Kaipago mark's, and nothing else changes:

  fur   (170,151,128) -> #9F958B
  face  (79,67,53)    -> #F4ECDC
  black               -> #1B1613

The vine, the swinging bodies and the other icons are left out.
