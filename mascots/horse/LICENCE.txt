WHAT WAS CHANGED

A different pack from the same artist as the wild animals, drawn to the same
scale and in the same side view, and converted the same way. The original ships
one PNG per animation as a horizontal strip at a 60x33 cell; kururu wants a
single sheet with a square cell and one clip per row:

  row 0   idle    (13 frames)
  row 1   walk    (8 frames)

Every frame is cropped to the union bounding box of both clips rather than to
its own, and then centred in a square 53px cell — see the fox's note for why
that is load-bearing and not tidiness.

The pack's run and eat strips are not carried. Two clips are what the badge has.

Nothing else was touched. No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
