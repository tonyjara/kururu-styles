WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is an eleven-by-eight grid of 48x32 cells holding eight animations —
death, idle hop, idle stand, idle peck, hop, take off, fly and land. Kururu wants
a square cell, so `sheet.png` here is two of those rows re-gridded:

  row 0   idle peck   (8 frames, row 3 of the original)
  row 1   fly         (9 frames, row 6 of the original)

Every frame is cropped to the union bounding box of both clips and centred in a
square cell, which is what keeps the standing bird standing and the flying bird
at its own height. Nothing was redrawn and no colour was changed.
