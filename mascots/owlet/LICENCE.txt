WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is a 4x4 grid of 16px cells on a checkerboard of two flat colours,
one per cell — idle, flying toward, flying away, and side-on. The only change
made here is that each cell's background was turned into transparency by
flood-filling from the cell's edge. It had to be a fill and not a colour key,
because the owl's pale belly is the same blue as half the checkerboard, and
keying it out punches a hole through every frame.

The grid is the artist's own and all four rows are still on the sheet, so
Settings can re-cut it — row 2 is the owl flying away, row 3 side-on. No pixel
was redrawn and no colour was changed.
