WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is one strip of twelve 15x20 frames: a three-frame walk, then a head
turned right, three views of the back of it, a head turned left, and the front
again. Kururu wants square cells and one clip per row, so each frame was placed
unchanged in a 20px cell, centred on the same two pixels for every frame, and the
strip was re-laid into two rows:

    row 0 (idle, 12 frames)   stand ×5, turn right, back ×3, turn left, stand ×2
    row 1 (working, 4 frames) stand, step, stand, other step

Frames are repeated rather than timed because a clip has one speed; holding the
front view for five frames is what makes the head-turn read as a glance and not
a spin. No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
