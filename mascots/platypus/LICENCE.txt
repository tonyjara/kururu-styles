WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original ships six separate 256x90 PNGs — a standing pose, a three-frame
walk and a two-frame jump. Kururu wants a single sheet with a square cell, one
clip per row, so `sheet.png` here lays them into a 247px grid:

  row 0   stand   (1 frame)    idle
  row 1   walk    (3 frames)   working
  row 2   jump    (2 frames)   unused, there for Settings to re-cut to

Every frame is cropped to the *union* bounding box of all six and then centred
in its cell, so the animal does not shift between frames.

**The trim is deliberately not the whole animal.** A platypus is three times
as long as it is tall, and a square around all of it at 16px is a brown line.
The trim is a 152px window over the front half instead — bill, head and the
forefoot that steps — and the rest is still in the cell for Settings to widen
to.

This is painted art, not pixel art, and it is downscaled smoothly rather than
by whole multiples. No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
