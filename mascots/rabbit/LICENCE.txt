WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original ships one PNG per animation, each a horizontal strip of frames at
that animal's own cell width. Kururu wants a single sheet with a square cell,
one clip per row, so `sheet.png` here is the pack's idle strip and its walk (or
run, or hop) strip laid into a two-row grid:

  row 0   idle
  row 1   working

Every frame is cropped to the *union* bounding box of both clips rather than to
its own, and then centred in a square cell. That is load-bearing rather than
tidy: a frame trimmed to its own content loses where the animal sits in its cell,
which is how a sheet draws a hop — trim each frame separately and every one of
them lands on the floor.

Nothing else was touched. No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
