WHAT WAS CHANGED

The same conversion the fox, wolf, bear, deer and rabbit in this repository got,
because this is the sixth animal in the pack they came from. The original ships
one PNG per animation, each a horizontal strip of frames at the animal's own
cell width; kururu wants a single sheet with a square cell and one clip per row:

  row 0   idle    (8 frames)
  row 1   walk    (8 frames)

Every frame is cropped to the *union* bounding box of both clips rather than to
its own, and then centred in a square 44px cell. That is load-bearing rather
than tidy: a frame trimmed to its own content loses where the animal sits in its
cell, which is how a sheet draws a gait — trim each frame separately and every
one of them lands on the floor.

The pack's run strip is not carried, for the same reason it is not carried for
the other five: two clips are what the badge has, and a third is weight on every
page load that nothing asks for.

Nothing else was touched. No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
