WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is not a grid at all: three animations laid out on one 400x400 sheet
at arbitrary offsets. `sheet.png` here is two of them re-gridded into square
cells, every frame cropped to the union bounding box of all of them:

  row 0   swim    (7 frames)
  row 1   breach  (5 frames)

**The trim is deliberately not the whole animal**, and this entry is the clearest
demonstration in the repository of what `trim` is for. An orca is nearly four
times as long as it is tall, so a square box around all of it renders a 16px
badge four pixels high — a dark smudge. The trim is a 32px window over its head
instead, so what the badge shows is an eye patch and a mouth line, bobbing. The
rest of the animal is still in the cell and Settings can widen the window.

`idle` is null, which is a plain dot. The breach on row 1 leaves the window when
the whale curls, and a mascot that disappears while its agent is *idle* would
lose the one distinction the badge exists to draw.
