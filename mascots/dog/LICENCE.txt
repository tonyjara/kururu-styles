WHAT WAS CHANGED

Almost nothing, and that is the point: the artist drew this on a 16x16 square
grid with one animation per row, which is already exactly what kururu wants. No
re-gridding, no cropping, no union bounding box — the cells here are the cells
they were drawn in, and at a 16px badge they are drawn at 1:1.

The one change is that the three pups ship as three separate PNGs upstream, and
here they are stacked into one:

  rows  0-4   white
  rows  5-9   brown
  rows 10-14  black

each keeping the artist's own row order — idle, walk, run, jump/fall, attack.
One sheet rather than three entries, on the fish's reasoning: a colour is not a
different mascot, and Settings can re-cut to any of the fifteen rows without
downloading anything.

White leads because of the badge rather than because of taste. Against kururu's
default chrome, which is nearly black, the black pup is a smudge and the brown
one is dim; the white one reads at 16px. The clips point at rows 0 and 1, and
the other two pups are one row change away.

No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed.
