WHAT WAS CHANGED

More than any other mascot in here, because the source is not a sheet in the
sense kururu means. It ships a static PNG of every pose packed at irregular
offsets, and two animated GIFs at 2x. The static sheet is the wrong thing to cut
from: its frames are *packed*, not placed, so the spacing between them is the
artist saving room rather than the cat moving, and its top row is a sheet of
poses — a front view, a side view, a sit — rather than a loop.

So the frames come from the GIFs, where a fixed canvas means every frame is
already aligned to every other, which is the thing that has to survive. Each was
halved back to its drawn size (the 2x is a clean nearest-neighbour double; this
was checked rather than assumed) and the flat purple matte keyed out.

  row 0   walk    (6 frames)
  row 1   run     (6 frames)

The two GIFs have different canvases — 18x15 and 20x17 — so there is no common
origin to inherit, and one had to be chosen. They are placed on the floor they
stand on and on a shared horizontal centre, then both cropped to the union
bounding box of all twelve frames and centred in a square 20px cell. Within a
clip every frame keeps its offset, which is where the walk actually lives.

`idle` is null, which is a plain dot. There is no idle animation to have: the
only other loop is the run, and a cat that sprints while its agent is *idle*
would say the opposite of the one thing the badge exists to say.

No pixel was redrawn. The only colour touched is the background matte, which was
made transparent.
