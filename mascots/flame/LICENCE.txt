WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is `messy.png`, and it is named honestly: every animation the
creature has, packed into one 786x326 sheet at whatever spacing fitted, plus two
GIFs at 3x and about 6.25x. Kururu wants a single sheet with a square cell, one
clip per row, so `sheet.png` lays them into a 92px grid:

  row 0   stand    (4 frames)   idle
  row 1   burn     (4 frames)   working
  row 2   walk     (7 frames)   the one in the artist's walking GIF
  row 3   shuffle  (7 frames)   an earlier, gentler walk on the same sheet
  row 4   ember    (8 frames)   the creature as a resting blob
  row 5   creep    (5 frames)   the blob sliding along
  row 6   blaze    (4 frames)   the bigger fire the creature turns into

Rows 2 to 6 are not used by default; they are there for Settings to re-cut to.
The one-shot transitions between forms are left out, because a badge loops.

The burn is taken from the artist's own GIF, halved back from its clean 3x, and
its loop is the last four of its five frames — the artist's note says to loop
exactly those, and the GIF does. Every other clip is cut from `messy.png`, where
frames are placed rather than packed: they stand on a shared floor at a regular
pitch, and that alignment was recovered by keeping each frame's body over the
first frame's. The same method was run against the two GIFs first, whose fixed
canvases are the artist's alignment, and it agreed to within one pixel.

Each clip is then cropped to the union bounding box of all its frames, stood on
the bottom of its cell and centred across. The trim is one square over every
frame of stand and burn together, which the burn's sparks decide.

No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed. Sparks between two frames on the
original sheet go to whichever frame they are nearer.
