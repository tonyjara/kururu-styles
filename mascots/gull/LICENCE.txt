WHAT WAS CHANGED

The original is a 32x32 grid holding two birds of five frames each, on a magenta
key colour. The only change made here is that the magenta was turned into
transparency; the grid is the artist's own and both birds are still on the sheet,
so Settings can re-cut it to the brown one on row 1.

Idle and working are the *same* five frames at different speeds, which is not a
shortcut so much as what a gliding bird is: there is no second animation on the
sheet worth having, and a bird frozen on one frame says exactly what the dot it
replaced said.
