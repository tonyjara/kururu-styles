WHAT WAS CHANGED

The sheet is the artist's, unmodified: a 32x32 grid of twelve colours, each with
eight animations. This entry points at the orange one — idle on row 8, swim on
row 9, columns 8 to 11 — and the other eleven colours are still there, which is
the whole reason the full sheet ships rather than a crop of it. Re-cutting to the
teal one is a drag in Settings.
