WHAT WAS CHANGED

The download (Coffee.zip) ships the steaming mug as a 16x32 animation in three
forms: a GIF, a sheet of its 13 frames, and a sheet of 28 frames in which some
are repeated. The repeats are the artist's timing, written out for engines that
play every frame for the same length of time, and kururu is one of those, so
the working clip is that 28-frame sequence as it stands:

    0 0 0 1 1 1 2 2 3 3 4 4 5 6 6 6 7 7 8 8 9 9 9 10 10 11 11 12

played at the GIF's own 100ms a frame, 2800ms a loop. Two curls of steam, one
leaning each way, with a beat of bare mug between them (frame 6).

Kururu wants one sheet with a square cell and one clip per row, so `sheet.png`
is a 32px grid with each 16x32 frame placed in the middle of a 32x32 cell, at
the height it had in its own:

  row 0   idle     (2 frames)   frame 6, the bare mug, then frame 0, the first
                                wisp — slowly, so the mug breathes one curl
  row 1   working  (28 frames)  the artist's sequence above

The idle clip is the only choice made here rather than recovered from the
download: the pack has one animation, and a mug that is waiting should still
look warm without looking busy. The trim is one square over every frame of both
clips, which the top of the highest curl decides.

No pixel was redrawn and no colour was changed. The 1px transparent gutter the
sheets put round each frame is dropped, and the static 16x16 mug and the
Aseprite files are left out.
